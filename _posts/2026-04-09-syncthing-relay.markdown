We now run a public [Syncthing](https://syncthing.net/) relay, helping Syncthing users connect their devices when a direct connection is not possible.
Relayed traffic stays end-to-end encrypted between your devices.
Live statistics are available on the [Syncthing relay list](https://relays.syncthing.net/), where our relay is listed as "cyberbits.dev".
