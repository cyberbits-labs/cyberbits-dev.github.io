Deployment

One command to publish

rsync is the main way to update your hosting. It only transfers what changed, so even large sites and mirrors update in seconds, and every transfer is encrypted.

  • rsync over SSH for fast, incremental, scriptable deploys
  • rsync over TLS with the standard rsync-ssl client
  • Fits any workflow: static site generators, CI jobs, cron or a shell alias
